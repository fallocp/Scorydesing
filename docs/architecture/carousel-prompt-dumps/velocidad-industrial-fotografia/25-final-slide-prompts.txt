DESIGN SPEC (shared by the whole set):
Editorial B2B photographic system for a premium international fintech brand, designed as a clean, white, institutional visual language with a single clear hero presence and lots of negative space. Composition should feel corporate, modern, global, and premium, with one dominant visual idea, maximum 1–3 secondary supporting elements, and generous empty space for later copy placement. Materials and surfaces should feel authentic and high-end, with matte, satin, ceramic-like, paper-like, brushed, or soft industrial finishes rather than glossy plastic. Typography is not present in the image, but the visual system should imply bold headline hierarchy through strong structural contrast and clear spatial organization. Avoid any sense of artificial urgency; the mood should be calm, controlled, and operationally fluid. White background style only, with a luminous, uncluttered, premium B2B look.
