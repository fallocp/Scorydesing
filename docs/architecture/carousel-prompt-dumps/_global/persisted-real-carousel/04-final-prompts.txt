===== SLIDE 1 — tension =====
CAROUSEL SLIDE 1 OF 5 — narrative role "tension". This image is one piece of a series. The DESIGN SPEC below is identical across every slide on purpose: keep the same visual family, the same recurring subject, the same camera treatment and the same palette. Only the narrative beat changes.

SUBJECT OF THIS SLIDE — it opens or closes the set, so the recurring subject is the protagonist here: Un motor industrial sobre una cotización de compra, convertido en el objeto que conecta el costo visible con su variación futura.

DESIGN SPEC (shared by the whole set):
Create a premium white-background Xending route-system visual for a 1:1 format, with no specific subject or scene, only an abstract industrial cost-flow metaphor. Use a clean operational-route diorama language expressed through a single continuous route, 2–4 physically coherent abstract milestones, and one clear hero focal structure. Convey the idea that changing FX rates can affect final industrial pricing through layered flow, conversion, and cost transmission, with a subtle tension-to-clarity progression: coral as the single point of pressure or cost sensitivity, teal as the active route and solution, navy as the structural framework. Composition must feel corporate, institutional, modern, technological, international, and B2B, with 80–90% white space, 6–12% navy structural elements, 2–5% teal route/activation, and 1–3% coral highlight only. Use refined white ceramic, matte white acrylic, satin light-gray and graphite details, precise rounded edges, soft contact shadows, and ultra-clean editorial rendering. Camera: elevated three-quarter/isometric product-style view with soft perspective, 45–70 mm equivalent lens feel, sharp hierarchy, and ample negative space for clarity. Lighting: bright studio softbox, natural clean reflections, controlled contrast, realistic shadow softness, no dark background dominance. Visual density must be minimal and premium: one route, one hero, at most 1–3 secondary nodes, no collage, no grid, no three-card layout, no clutter. Since textInImage is false, include no readable text, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermarks, only universal non-text symbols such as subtle arrows, check-like marks, pause-like status cues, and abstract nodes. The overall system should feel like a polished financial infrastructure diagram, not a generic infographic, not Canva, not crypto, not cyberpunk, not toy-like, and not a literal scene.
