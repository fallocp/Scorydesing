===== SLIDE 1 — tension =====
CAROUSEL SLIDE 1 OF 5 — narrative role "tension". This image is one piece of a series. The DESIGN SPEC below is identical across every slide on purpose: keep the same visual family, the same recurring subject, the same camera treatment and the same palette. Only the narrative beat changes.

SUBJECT OF THIS SLIDE — it opens or closes the set, so the recurring subject is the protagonist here: Dos estados de cuenta comparables abren la serie y un único resultado operativo la cierra.

DESIGN SPEC (shared by the whole set):
Premium 3D iconography / product-visual system on a pure white background, designed as a corporate B2B comparison layout with two clean vertical columns and a clear left-to-right reading order. The composition should communicate comparison and operational outcome through abstract, polished 3D forms only: one side in neutral grays representing the status quo, the other side in brand colors representing the improved flow. Use a single hero visual hierarchy with 1–3 secondary elements maximum, large areas of negative space, and a clean editorial grid. Camera in a slightly elevated three-quarter view with soft isometric discipline, 45–70 mm equivalent feel, subtle perspective, no dramatic distortion. Materials must feel premium and tactile: matte white ceramic, satin acrylic, brushed light graphite, and refined soft-edge surfaces with precise bevels. Lighting is bright studio softbox, high-key but not overexposed, with gentle contact shadows and controlled reflections. Palette distribution: 80–90% white (#FFFFFF) and very light gray (#F5F5F5), 6–12% navy (#0F1419) for structure, outlines, and checks, 2–5% teal (#2ED4C7) for the active/solved state, and 1–3% coral (#FF7A4A) for the waiting/problem state only. Keep the left side visually restrained and neutral, the right side cleaner and more activated with brand color accents. The layout should feel like a premium comparison table translated into 3D, with crisp alignment, corporate clarity, and a single strong operational result at the end. Typography only if absolutely necessary, and only as exact provided copy; otherwise no text. If text is present, use Montserrat Bold/ExtraBold for headline and Poppins Medium/SemiBold for body and CTA, flat vector-like, matte, clean, and fully legible. Maintain a modern institutional B2B aesthetic, lots of breathing room, minimal secondary detail, no clutter, no decorative excess, and no aggressive contrast.

- Risk can also be carried by a thin line, a small arrow, a percentage, a stamp or a border. Coral used with restraint reads far more sophisticated than coral used as a highlighter.

- Current exchange rate: turquoise.
- Future illustrative exchange rate: coral.
- Current cost in MXN: navy or turquoise.
- Future cost in MXN: coral.
- Difference and percentage: coral.
